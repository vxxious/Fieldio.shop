Purpose: Present Fieldio as a premium worldwide fashion sourcing and shopping service, then guide customers from discovery to a clear WhatsApp order request.
Audience: Fashion-conscious customers seeking women, men, accessories, sportswear, and luxury pieces, including personal-shopping clients who already know a brand or item they want sourced.
Primary action: Browse the Fieldio edit and select a product or sourcing request.
Secondary actions: Open the personal-shopping service, explore a luxury brand, join the mailing list, or continue an order request on WhatsApp.
Mood: Minimal, editorial, assured, globally minded, and warm without feeling ornamental.
Visual direction: Follow the selected Catalog Index reference: compact centered masthead, oversized opening typography, flush image-led product grid, thin rules, stark whitespace, neutral palette, rigorous alignment, and unrounded controls. Use Fieldio-generated editorial imagery and no third-party luxury logos or copied catalog photography.
Typography: Display-scale sans serif headlines with tight tracking; compact sans serif utility copy and metadata; restrained hierarchy with sentence case.
Color: Warm off-white paper, near-black ink, muted warm grays, oxblood and olive only through photography, and no gradients.
Motion: Fast editorial reveals, purposeful drawer and menu transitions, subtle product image movement, and full reduced-motion support.
Responsive behavior: Four-column desktop index becomes a compact two-column mobile catalog; editorial split sections stack intentionally; controls remain touch-friendly; the product purchase action becomes sticky on mobile.
Content constraints: Keep the catalog mixed across women, men, bags, shoes, and luxury sourcing. Louis Vuitton is prominent as a sourcing service, but do not claim affiliation, scrape branded imagery, invent prices, or imply payment is complete.
